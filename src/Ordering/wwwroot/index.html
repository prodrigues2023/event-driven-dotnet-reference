Order fulfilment / event-driven console

live · localhost:8080

Place an order and watch it move across three services by events alone — Ordering → Payments → Shipping. No service calls another directly; each reacts to an event and emits the next. Failures are visible, not lost: over-limit orders are declined, malformed ones are dead-lettered.
